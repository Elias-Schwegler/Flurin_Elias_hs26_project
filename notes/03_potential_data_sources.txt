DSPRO1 – HS26 – Data Sources  Following Links summarize some sources for energy data on di7erent topics and from di7erent scales. Before combining datasets, check that their spatial units, boundary versions, time periods, coordinate systems and licences are compatible. 
1. Energy data sources These sources directly describe energy production, consumption, infrastructure, prices, trade, technologies or emissions from energy use. 
Switzerland • Swiss Federal Office of Energy: energy statistics — official energy balances, electricity statistics, renewable energy, prices and sector-specific publications. • Swiss Energy Dashboard — recent electricity, gas, price, weather and supply-security indicators, with links to open data and an API. • opendata.swiss: energy datasets — federal, cantonal and municipal energy datasets in one catalogue. • Swissgrid grid data — transmission-system load, production, cross-border exchange and ancillary-service data. • ElCom electricity tariffs — electricity prices and tariff components by municipality, grid operator and customer category. 
Europe • Eurostat energy database — harmonised energy balances, supply, final consumption, prices, renewables and efficiency. • ENTSO-E Transparency Platform — generation, load, prices, cross-border flows, outages and balancing data for European electricity systems. • ENTSO-G Transparency Platform — gas flows, capacities, network points, LNG and storage data. • European Commission Joint Research Centre Data Catalogue — research datasets on energy technologies, buildings, transport, emissions and renewable potential. • Open Power System Data — cleaned European electricity time series, power-plant lists, weather inputs and household load profiles. • Ember Electricity Data Explorer — harmonised electricity generation, demand, imports and power-sector emissions. 
Global • International Energy Agency data and statistics — country energy balances, indicators and topic-specific datasets; access conditions vary. 
• UN Energy Statistics — official national production, trade, transformation and consumption data. • Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy — global time series on fuels, electricity, trade, prices, emissions and technologies. • IRENA data — renewable capacity, generation, costs, finance and employment. • U.S. Energy Information Administration Open Data — detailed U.S. and international energy statistics. • Our World in Data: Energy and its downloadable dataset — harmonised country-level energy and electricity indicators assembled from multiple sources. • energydata.info — datasets on electricity access, networks, utilities, demand and renewable resources. • Global Energy Monitor trackers — unit-level power stations, mines, pipelines and other energy infrastructure. • Nature Scientific Data on Energy — unit-level power stations, mines, pipelines and other energy infrastructure. 
2. Complementary data to merge with energy data These sources can provide explanatory variables, denominators, spatial context or outcome measures. The most useful merge keys are commonly a building identiﬁer, coordinates, municipality or statistical-area code, and date or timestamp. 
Buildings and addresses • Swiss Federal Register of Buildings and Dwellings — building coordinates, category, construction period, dwellings and heating characteristics where publishable. • FSO building statistics — aggregated building stock, dwelling and heating information. • City of Zurich Open Data — detailed georeferenced building, entrance and dwelling records for Zurich. • EU Building Stock Observatory — European building stock, systems, consumption, renovation and energy-performance indicators. • OpenStreetMap — global building footprints, land uses, roads, amenities and infrastructure under the ODbL. 
Population, households and socioeconomic conditions • FSO STAT-TAB — Swiss population, households, income, employment, economic activity and regional statistics. • Swiss population and household statistics — demographic and household variables with official geographic classifications. • Eurostat database — European population, income, housing, industry and regional indicators. • World Bank Open Data — country population, income, development, access and economic indicators. • Global Human Settlement Layer — gridded population, built-up area and settlement classifications worldwide. 
Weather and climate • MeteoSwiss Open Data — Swiss station observations, climate series and gridded products. • Copernicus Climate Data Store and ERA5 — global hourly reanalysis of temperature, wind, radiation, precipitation and other variables. • NASA POWER — global solar and meteorological time series through an API. • NOAA climate data — global station, climate and extreme-weather records. 
Land use, terrain and spatial constraints • swisstopo geodata — Swiss topography, elevation, imagery, boundaries and base maps. • FSO land-use statistics — land cover and land-use change across Switzerland. • Copernicus Land Monitoring Service — European and global land cover, urban form and vegetation products. • Copernicus Urban Atlas — detailed land use for European urban areas. • OpenStreetMap — roads, buildings, land use and infrastructure for accessibility and proximity analysis. 
Mobility and transport • Open Transport Data Switzerland — public-transport stops, schedules, real-time movements and related datasets. • Swiss federal electric-mobility information — information and data links concerning electric vehicles and charging infrastructure. • European Alternative Fuels Observatory — European vehicles, charging and alternative-fuel infrastructure. • Eurostat transport database — vehicles, passenger and freight activity, modal split and transport infrastructure. • OpenStreetMap — road networks, cycle infrastructure, parking, charging locations and accessibility variables. 
Economy, industry and prices • FSO national economy statistics — Swiss GDP, production, employment and sector structure. • Eurostat database — GDP, industrial activity, prices, trade and regional economic indicators. • OECD Data Explorer — economic activity, industry, prices, taxation and environmental indicators. • World Bank Open Data — GDP, industrial structure, trade, population and price indicators for global comparisons. 
Environment, emissions and health • FOEN environmental data — Swiss greenhouse gases, air pollution, water, biodiversity, noise and environmental indicators. • FOEN hydrological data — river flow, lake levels and water temperature for hydropower and cooling studies. 
• European Environment Agency Datahub — European emissions, air quality, climate and environmental indicators. • UNFCCC greenhouse-gas data — official national greenhouse-gas inventories. • EDGAR emissions database — global gridded and country emissions by sector and pollutant. • Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service — atmospheric composition and air-quality products. • Protected Planet and GBIF — protected areas and species-occurrence data for ecological constraints.
